02. Data Fidelity

SOURCE: ORACLE NETWORK
Δ

MODEL VS. STATION

Precipitation data relies on satellite/radar models (e.g., GPM IMERG) processed by decentralized oracles. Model precipitation does not equal ground station measurements. Local microclimates may experience rain not reflected in the macro-model data grid.

±

GRID RESOLUTION

Data is aggregated over standardized spatial grids (typically 0.1° x 0.1° or ~10km x 10km). A localized event occurring partially within a grid cell is averaged across the entire cell area, potentially dampening peak intensity readings.

RECALIBRATED FINAL T+72 Hours
SETTLEMENT WINDOW Post-Finality Only

Settlement relies strictly on final recalibrated data feeds. Provisional feeds are illustrative only.

03. Execution Risk

ON-CHAIN DYNAMICS
Oracle Failure

If the designated data oracle fails to report or reports demonstrably corrupt data (as defined by contract invariants), settlement is suspended indefinitely until manual intervention via DAO governance.

Liquidity Shortfall

Payouts are mathematically capped by the liquidity available in the specific pool backing the contract at the exact time of settlement. Under-collateralization events (though guarded against) result in pro-rata settlement.

Smart Contract Risk

While audited, the protocol inherently carries risk of exploitation or unforeseen execution errors. Users interact with the bytecode 'as-is' without legal recourse against the developers.
